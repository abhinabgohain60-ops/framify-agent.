# Script Rules

## Opening hooks (Beat 1)
- Prioritize curiosity, pattern-interrupts, or problem statements that match proven retention formats.
- Avoid generic greetings; lead with tension or benefit in first 3-7 words when possible.

## Retention mechanics
- Each scene must advance to the next beat with a micro-commitment (hook payoff or curiosity gap).
- Trim filler words and pauses > 0.5s from recorded takes; prefer last take of repeated lines.

## Competitive benchmarking
- When topic is given, benchmark against 3-5 viral shorts/reels in the exact niche before scripting.
- Synthesize an original script that refines and counters benchmarked hooks.
